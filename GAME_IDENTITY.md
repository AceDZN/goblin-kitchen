# Goblin Kitchen

Core verbs: move, grab, carry, peel, slice, measure, stir, toss, serve, rearrange.

60 seconds: read in a paused kitchen, take a banana, peel it at the board, carry it into the bowl, measure two tablespoons, gently stir and launch the dish at a waiting customer. The next shift changes the recipe, ingredient form and route.

Decision: interpret the English noun, amount and prepared state, then choose a physical route and action. State lives in the held item, bowl contents, tool, cleanliness and completed preparation. An extra spoon produces wobbling yogurt; scoop it out or reset the bowl. Bad service costs tips and creates a splat, with unlimited recovery. Win all five shifts, ending in two new apples and a new kitchen layout with no recipe hint.

Camera: oblique overhead orthographic, rectangular kitchen filling the scene. Silhouette: short green cook, long ears, apron; chunky produce; tall amber hatch. Materials: worn wood, cream ceramic, brass and aubergine tiles. Color: warm orange light, aubergine, herb green, cream. Sound: footsteps, chop, spoon, stirring and rising service chime, synthesized after user gesture.

Visual thesis: a warm crooked street kitchen, tactile wood and ceramic, readable chaos.
Content plan: the playfield is the full-bleed anchor, one pinned recipe, a slim shift bar and contextual controls.
Interaction thesis: carried ingredients bob, food visibly changes during prep, the served dish arcs through the hatch and customers cheer. Reduced motion removes bobbing and particles.
