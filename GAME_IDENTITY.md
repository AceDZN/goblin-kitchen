# Goblin Kitchen — 36-level journey

Core verbs: move, grab, carry, peel, slice, dice, grate, measure, toast, roast, stir, blend, park, toss, serve, rearrange.

The opening minute still teaches one physical loop: read in a paused kitchen, take a banana, peel it, carry it into the bowl, measure yogurt, stir and toss the dish through the hatch. This is the beginning of a campaign, not the entire game.

The journey contains 36 authored levels and 60 orders across six places: Ember Alley, the Bread Yard, Garden Market, Harbour Canteen, Rooftop Lab and the Royal Feast. Advancing unlocks new ingredients, tools and methods. Difficulty grows through mixed ingredients, preparation/heat distinctions, measuring different liquids, explicit layers, multiple independent bowls and ordered service. Later recipes demand longer connected English instructions and careful planning. The campaign has no school-grade selector or grade-dependent content restriction.

A successful level opens the next. A local save retains completed levels, best stars and the last played level. Replays and a current-level retry are available without clearing progress. The host's one-based ?level=N link can open any of the 36 levels directly. Reading pauses action; pace targets award an optional action star but cannot prevent completion. Help and first-attempt comprehension remain separate from action stars.

The player interprets ingredient names, quantities, state, methods, before/after relations and course order, then chooses a physical route. Authoritative state lives in held ingredients, per-order bowls/trays, source counts, measured contents, heat and processing history. Rejected dishes return for repair; wrong ingredients/layers or an unwanted blend can be washed and remade. No quiz gates replace cooking.

Visual thesis: a warm travelling goblin kitchen, tactile wood and ceramic, with each new venue changing the skyline, materials, decor and route while preserving readable controls. The full-canvas playfield remains the anchor; one pinned English order and small contextual actions provide information. The level map is a calm, scrollable route of six chapters, not a grade menu.

Interaction thesis: the actual Tripo cook deforms through idle/walk/carry clips; held props follow hand bones, ingredient silhouettes change with preparation, tools produce visible heat/texture changes, and bowls arc to a customer before scoring. Reduced motion removes decorative motion. Recipe/help/pause freeze cooking and flights.

Camera: elevated orthographic overview, adapted for portrait. Materials: worn wood, cream patterned ceramic, brass and venue-specific floor/awning colors. Original procedural food/tool/decor geometry supports precise authored portions/colliders; the original rigged Tripo cook and bowl remain bundled locally.
