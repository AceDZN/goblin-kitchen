# Goblin Kitchen curriculum mapping — 36-level campaign

Goblin Kitchen is one regular progression of 36 levels across six kitchens. There is no school-grade selector or grade gate. The campaign uses original English recipes based on the two saved Workbook lessons below. Their source URLs identify the curriculum scope; they do not classify the player or unlock content by grade.

- **R — Reading Recipes and Essential Steps:** [Workbook lesson](https://english.workbook.co.il/grade-7/chapter-4/read-and-explain-a-recipe). Imperative action/object, ingredient state, first/next/then/finally, and the referent in “the pear that you prepared.” Saved snapshot: `docs/curriculum/grade-7_chapter-4_read-and-explain-a-recipe.md`.
- **Q — Food Quantities and Containers:** [Workbook lesson](https://english.workbook.co.il/grade-7/chapter-4/how-much-do-we-need). Counted heads such as slice/loaf, measured contents versus units/containers, exact quantities, and a little without an exact number. Saved snapshot: `docs/curriculum/grade-7_chapter-4_how-much-do-we-need.md`.

Dice, grate, toast, roast, blend, additional vegetable names, broth, oil, teaspoon and half-cup are original scenario extensions. They reuse the action/object, prepared-state, sequence and quantity distinctions; these two snapshots are not claimed to teach every added word. The game does not claim Ministry approval, speaking assessment, measured learning gains, complete coverage of a school year, or productive mastery of much/many merely because a player counts food.

## How the campaign preserves meaning

- Food identity, preparation form, source count and heat are separate state. Slicing two apples still represents two source apples; slicing one bread loaf yields four counted slices. A slice is not a loaf.
- Tablespoons, teaspoons, cups and half-cup scoops are labeled deterministic game measures. Liquid name and unit are checked separately. A little yogurt accepts a small positive amount up to four game tablespoons in equivalent volume, including smaller teaspoon portions; it never implies a secret exact amount or an unstated required measuring tool. The quantities describe fictional cooking, not nutrition or health advice.
- Independent preparation may happen in either order. Only explicit food-group layers and the stated serving brief impose additional order. The English never requires a hidden liquid-before-food action or an unimplemented timing trick.
- Blending acts on the carried whole dish after its additions; adding more afterward requires a new blend. Chunky soup explicitly retains pieces. Raw/toasted/roasted distinctions are consequential.
- English task text stays LTR inside the Hebrew shell. Reading pauses play. Optional vocabulary pauses play and marks assistance. Default venue flavor text does not translate the full recipe. Level 5 has no solution hint.
- Only level 1 is guided practice. Other levels can record a first unaided attempt when no current/prior help, mistake or repeated exposure disqualifies it. Replay/restart does not restore that credit. Stars separately reward completion, no corrections and a soft pace target; the target never prevents completion.
- MIX trays preserve each order's ingredient count/form. Course order is enforced only when the English brief states it. The pantry includes every required ingredient and plausible distractors, with bread at its own station.

## Progression and review evidence

The opening five recipe fixtures remain exact, including the clarified transfer wording: “Slice two apples and put the slices in a clean bowl. Add three tablespoons of yogurt. Then stir and serve.” Joining prep/place with and permits either apple to be prepared and added first; the final three tablespoons and subsequent stir remain required.

New actions arrive gradually: teaspoon at 6; toast at 7; dice at 9; grate at 10; half-cup/broth at 14; roast at 15; blend at 17; three concurrent courses at 18; multiple liquid measures at 20; three ingredient layers at 21. Later venues recombine those distinctions, change layouts and service relations, and culminate in three courses at level 36. Counts remain bounded at 1–3 per ingredient rather than manufacturing difficulty through giant quantities.

`tests/campaign.test.ts` solves every level through actual Kitchen actions, then completes one continuous 1→36 run. It independently checks near misses for units, liquid identity/amounts, form/heat, layers, final blending and ordered service, plus save/replay bounds. The combined suite has 78 passing tests and typecheck passes, including the available-unit volume boundary for a little yogurt. This is evidence of rule solvability, not player enjoyment or learning gains. New browser/visual evidence is recorded in `docs/REVIEW.md`; the previous five-level release captures are historical and do not by themselves verify the expanded campaign.
